# New York City Demo Transit — Service change note

**Subject:** GTFS update for the November 2026 publication
**From:** Service Planning
**To:** Passenger Data

Hello,

Please update the network's GTFS for the next publication. The changes below apply over the whole validity of the feed.

## 1. Route B2 (14 St Crosstown) — Madison Sq no longer served

1. Route B2 no longer serves the **Madison Sq** stop, in both directions and on every day (weekdays, Saturday, Sunday), because of long-term street works.
2. The stop itself stays: route S1 keeps serving it.
3. B2 times at every other stop stay as they are, Herald Square and Union Square included.

## 2. East River Ferry F1 — Sunday mornings

1. On Sundays, the first F1 departure is at **9:00** in each direction (from Wall St / Pier 11 and from East 34 St Ferry).
2. The Sunday departures before 9:00 are withdrawn: 7:30, 8:00 and 8:30, in both directions.
3. Sunday times from 9:00, and the weekday and Saturday times, do not change.

## 3. Route B1 (LaGuardia Link SBS) — headsign

1. Towards Chelsea Market, the headsign now reads **Chelsea Market via Grand Central** instead of "Chelsea Market", on every trip of every day.
2. The direction towards LaGuardia Terminal B does not change.

## 4. The rest of the network

1. No other route changes: S1, S2, S3, B4, B5, B6 and N1 stay as they are.

Thank you,
Service Planning
